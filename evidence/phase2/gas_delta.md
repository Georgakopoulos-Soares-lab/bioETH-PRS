# Local gas measurements for fixed thresholds

## What was evaluated

The values below were measured in the local simulation using the **Classic method (stored
inputs)**. Models in this table were published without stored category thresholds; storing them
used a separate transaction and 77,314 gas.

## Results

| Variants | Model publication | Calculation creation | Upload | Calculation | Return result | Total |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 | 1,084,966 | 301,270 | 10,287,793 | 5,626,326 | 186,998 | 17,487,353 |
| 300 | 2,646,740 | 301,270 | 30,556,438 | 16,808,466 | 186,998 | 50,499,912 |
| 600 | 4,989,383 | 301,270 | 61,097,217 | 33,581,676 | 186,998 | 100,156,544 |

## What the results mean

These are local contract measurements. They do not measure Sepolia fees, live FHE speed, or
production cost.
